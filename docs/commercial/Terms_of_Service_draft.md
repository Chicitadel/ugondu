Where valid consent is executed at checkout and confirmed on a durable medium, the right ceases upon the Activation Event or commencement of digital supply.

L. 221-25):** For continuous verification subscriptions and cloud services, the Consumer may request immediate execution; the right of withdrawal remains preserved during the 14-day window, but if exercised, the Consumer owes a **pro-rata amount** for services rendered up to withdrawal notification.
