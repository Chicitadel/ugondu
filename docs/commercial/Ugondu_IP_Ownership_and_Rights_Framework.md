## 1. Executive Summary and Constitutional Purpose

This Master Framework establishes the corporate governance baseline for the allocation of intellectual property rights, moral rights, economic exploitation rights, and licensing boundaries governing the EAORCS Platform and associated technologies. It serves as the binding internal constitutional bridge harmonizing:

This document serves as the authoritative source of truth. In the event of any contradiction regarding intellectual property ownership between this Framework and any commercial customer agreement (EULA, Terms of Service, Order Form), this Framework shall strictly govern internal corporate reality.

## 5. French Chain-of-Title Compliance (Article L. 131-3 CPI)

Under Article L. 131-3 of the French *Code de la propriété intellectuelle*, any transmission or assignment of authors' economic rights requires strict formal delimitation:
1. **Identification of Rights:** Each assigned exploitation right (reproduction, representation, adaptation, translation) must be explicitly mentioned in writing.
2. **Delimitation of Scope:** The transfer must precisely state the field of exploitation, territorial scope, purpose, and duration.
3. **Prohibition on Global Future Assignments:** Article L. 131-1 of the French CPI renders null and void any blanket assignment of future works (*la cession globale des œuvres futures est nulle*).

### 5.1 Internal Title Perfection
Air Roofers SASU intends to maintain and record formal written contribution agreements, founder assignments, and software transfer deeds complying strictly with Articles L. 131-2 and L. 131-3 of the French CPI (including the Founder Contribution Deed AR-IP-2025-01 currently pending formal execution). Commercial customer contracts shall not attempt to construct informal, blanket future assignments from employees or customers, but shall rely on verified written deeds.

## 6. Public Sector, Government, and Sovereign Governance

In strict alignment with the principles established across the AFFIANTOR, CIVISCORE, CONSUNEXIA, and GOVINSIGHT declarations:

6.1 **No Implied Transfer Through Public Participation:** Public procurement, government tenders, public-sector pilots, state funding, or regulatory auditing **do not constitute an assignment or transfer of intellectual property rights** in the EAORCS Platform to any government or public authority.

6.2 **Public-Sector Use is Licensed Use:** Deployments within governmental agencies, ministries, or sovereign defense infrastructure operate strictly under licensed grants governed by the standard EULA supplemented by the **Enterprise & Sovereign Addendum**.

6.3 **Public Records and Freedom of Information:** Customer audit reports and compliance certificates generated for public authorities may constitute public or administrative records to the extent required by applicable law, without transferring title to the underlying software engine, verification algorithms, or proprietary source code. Economic rights in the Platform are validly vested in AIR ROOFERS, subject to third-party rights, open-source licences, and founder moral rights.

## 8. Founder Protection and Reversion Architecture

8.1 **Separation of Corporate Governance from Customer EULAs:** The internal IP declarations contain critical conditional reversion mechanisms designed to preserve founder continuity in extraordinary corporate contingencies (e.g., insolvency, hostile change of control, corporate dissolution).

8.2 **Drafting Rule for Customer Contracts:** While these founder protections are legally binding between the Founder and Air Roofers SASU under internal corporate pacts, **they must not be promised in customer EULAs as defeating mandatory insolvency proceedings, statutory liquidators, or secured creditors under French commercial law**. Customer agreements shall simply state:

> *"Nothing in this Agreement grants the Customer any ownership interest in the intellectual property of Air Roofers SASU or any underlying rights of the originating author. Internal ownership, corporate succession, and continuity arrangements applicable to such intellectual property are governed exclusively by separate instruments and applicable French law."*

This approach eliminates contractual vulnerability while maintaining complete founder protection.
