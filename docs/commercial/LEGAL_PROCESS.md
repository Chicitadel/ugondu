# Legal Process

## Source Documents
All legal documents located in `docs/commercial/` are considered **source documents only**.

## Human-Controlled Processes
The following processes are strictly manual and human-controlled. This process is **NOT** automated by any platform system:
- Legal review
- Author signature
- Legal counsel signature
- Timestamping
- Official deposit

## Official Publication
`policies.airroofers.eu/ugondu/` serves only the approved, signed, and deposited versions of the documents.
Please reference `platform-core/policies.airroofers.eu/` as the definitive publication authority.
