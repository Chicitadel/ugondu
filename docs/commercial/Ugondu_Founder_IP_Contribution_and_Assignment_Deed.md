## Article 2 — Recitals

**2.1** Ignatus Chika Ujomor is the originating author, system architect, and primary developer of the EAORCS platform — a software and intellectual property work conceived, designed, and created in his personal capacity prior to and in the course of founding AIR ROOFERS SASU.

**2.2** Under French *Code de la propriété intellectuelle* Article L. 111-1, original works of the mind vest by operation of law in their author from the moment of creation. The Assignor is accordingly the originating holder of all copyright subsisting in the EAORCS Works as defined in this Deed.

**2.3** French CPI Article L. 121-1 confers on the author perpetual, inalienable, and imprescriptible moral rights (*droit au respect de son nom, de sa qualité et de son œuvre*) which the Assignor retains absolutely and cannot transfer by this or any other instrument.

**2.4** The Assignor and the Assignee wish to formally constitute, on the terms set out in this Deed, an assignment of economic exploitation rights (*droits patrimoniaux*) over the EAORCS Works to AIR ROOFERS, to enable the Assignee to commercialise, license, distribute, and maintain the EAORCS platform under a coherent corporate IP structure.

**2.5** This Deed is intended to comply with French CPI Articles L. 131-2 (written form requirement), L. 131-3 (mandatory delimitation of each assigned right, territory, duration, and purpose), and L. 131-4 (remuneration framework applicable to software works).

## Article 4 — Assigned Economic Rights

**4.1** Subject to the reservations in Article 5, the Assignor hereby assigns to the Assignee, with effect from formal execution of this Deed:

**(a) Right of Reproduction** (*droit de reproduction*) — CPI Art. L. 122-3:  
The right to reproduce or authorise reproduction of the EAORCS Works in any form, including digital, electronic, or machine-readable form, whether in whole or in part, directly or indirectly.

**(b) Right of Representation / Communication** (*droit de représentation*) — CPI Art. L. 122-2:  
The right to make the EAORCS Works available to the public by any technical means, including online platform deployment, API delivery, cloud services, SaaS, and interactive transmission.

**(c) Right of Adaptation, Translation & Transformation** (*droit de traduction, d'adaptation*) — CPI Art. L. 122-4:  
The right to create derivative works, translations, adaptations, ports, updates, and new versions of the EAORCS Works.

**(d) Right of Commercial Distribution** (*droit de distribution*) — CPI Art. L. 122-1:  
The right to commercialise, sell, lease, sublicense, and distribute the EAORCS Works and derivative products in all commercial forms, including SaaS, thin-client installation, OEM, and managed service models.

**(e) Right of Sublicensing** (*droit de sous-licence*):  
The right to grant end-user licenses, enterprise licenses, government addenda, and subprocessor arrangements on terms determined by the Assignee within the limits of this Deed.

**4.2** These rights are assigned on the following terms:

## Article 5 — Reservations and Exclusions

**5.1 Moral Rights (Strictly Reserved)**  
The Assignor's moral rights (*droit moral*) under French CPI Article L. 121-1 are permanent, inalienable, and imprescriptible. They are not transferred by this Deed and cannot be waived. These include:
- Right of disclosure (*droit de divulgation*) — CPI Art. L. 121-2;
- Right of authorship and attribution (*droit à la paternité*) — CPI Art. L. 121-1;
- Right of integrity (*droit au respect de l'œuvre*) — CPI Art. L. 121-1;
- Right of reconsideration and retraction (*droit de repentir ou de retrait*) — CPI Art. L. 121-4.

All EAORCS commercial documents, end-user licences, and public-facing materials shall preserve the Assignor's authorship attribution in conformance with this reservation.

**5.2 Future Works**  
Pursuant to French CPI Article L. 131-1, no assignment of future works is effected by this Deed beyond the EAORCS Works as defined in Article 3. Future original works created by the Assignor shall require a separate written instrument to constitute an effective assignment.

**5.3 Customer Materials and Data**  
Nothing in this Deed affects customer ownership of Customer Data, Customer Materials, or Customer-specific evidence generated through EAORCS platform operations. Customer rights are governed exclusively by commercial contracts between AIR ROOFERS and the relevant customer.

**5.4 Open-Source Components**  
Open-source software components incorporated into the EAORCS platform are governed by their respective open-source licences and are expressly excluded from this Deed.

## Article 6 — Remuneration

**6.1** Pursuant to French CPI Article L. 131-4, which establishes that authors are entitled to proportional participation in the revenues generated from exploitation of their works (with specific provisions applicable to software works regarding the *rémunération forfaitaire* framework), the agreed consideration structure for this assignment is:

> *(a)* The Assignor is the sole *Président* and sole shareholder of AIR ROOFERS SASU, the Assignee. The economic benefit of the assignment flows to the Assignor through his 100% beneficial ownership of the Assignee.  
>  
> *(b)* The specific consideration structure — whether structured as initial consideration, deferred remuneration, dividend policy, or a combination — shall be formally determined and documented by qualified French legal counsel and corporate accounting advisors, having regard to CPI Art. L. 131-4 requirements, French corporate tax treatment, and the Assignor's specific circumstances.  
>  
> *(c)* Until such formal determination is completed and documented, no representation is made that the remuneration structure has been legally validated. This clause shall be a mandatory item for Gate 7 (Human Legal Review) under the EAORCS Remediation Plan v0.5.
