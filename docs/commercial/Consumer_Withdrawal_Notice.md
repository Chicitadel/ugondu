## Part A: Statutory Notice on the Right of Withdrawal
*(Informations concernant l'exercice du droit de rétractation)*

### 1. Statutory Right of Withdrawal
Under Article L. 221-18 of the French *Code de la consommation*, if you are contracting as a Consumer (a natural person acting for purposes which are outside your trade, business, craft, or profession), you have the right to withdraw from this contract without giving any reason within **fourteen (14) calendar days**.

The withdrawal period expires fourteen (14) days after the day of the conclusion of the contract.

### 2. Supply of Digital Content Not on a Tangible Medium (Article L. 221-28, 13°)
Under Article L. 221-28, 13° of the French *Code de la consommation*, for contracts for the supply of **digital content** not supplied on a tangible medium (such as downloadable EAORCS standalone binaries, license keys, or static verification schemas):
- The right of withdrawal **cannot be exercised** if performance has begun after you have given your **prior express consent** and expressly **acknowledged that you will lose your right of withdrawal**;
- If you ticked the dedicated consent checkbox at checkout requesting immediate supply and acknowledging waiver, your right of withdrawal ceases as soon as digital delivery or the Activation Event occurs;
- Confirmation of your contract and express consent is provided on a durable medium (*support durable*) via electronic confirmation and invoice.

### 2bis. Supply of Digital Services (Article L. 221-25)
Under Article L. 221-25 of the French *Code de la consommation*, for contracts for the supply of **digital services** (such as continuous verification subscriptions, hosted telemetry processing, or managed assurance pipelines):
- If you request that the service begin before the end of the 14-day withdrawal period, you must make an express request at checkout;
- **Your right of withdrawal is preserved** during the 14-day period until the service has been fully performed;
- If you exercise your right of withdrawal during the 14-day period while service performance is ongoing, you are liable to pay Air Roofers an amount calculated **pro rata** to the service provided up to the time you communicated your withdrawal, in comparison with the full contractual price;
- The right of withdrawal ceases once the digital service has been fully performed, provided performance began following your express request and acknowledgement of loss upon full performance.

### 2ter. Mixed Supply (Bundled Content and Continuous Service)
Where an EAORCS subscription tier combines standalone digital content with continuous digital services, the rules in §2 apply to the digital content component, and the rules in §2bis apply to the ongoing service component.

### 3. Exercising the Right of Withdrawal
Where the statutory right of withdrawal remains exercisable (for instance, if digital content performance has not commenced with valid waiver, or for ongoing digital services within 14 days), you may exercise your right by notifying us of your decision through an unambiguous declaration prior to the expiration of the 14-day period:

- **By Email:** `withdrawals@airroofers.eu`
- **By Postal Mail:**  
  **AIR ROOFERS SASU — Service Rétractations**  
  229 rue Saint-Honoré, 75001 Paris, France  

You may use the attached Model Withdrawal Form (Part B), although its use is not mandatory. To meet the withdrawal deadline, it is sufficient for you to send your communication before the 14-day period has expired.

### 4. Effects of Withdrawal
If you validly withdraw from this contract:
1. **Digital Content / Unperformed Services:** We shall reimburse all payments received from you without undue delay and in any event not later than **fourteen (14) calendar days** from the day on which we are informed of your decision to withdraw.
2. **Ongoing Digital Services Requested Early:** The reimbursement will equal the total amount paid minus the pro-rata portion corresponding to the service rendered up to the date of withdrawal.
3. **Reimbursement Method:** Reimbursement will be executed using the same payment method used for the initial transaction, unless you expressly agree otherwise; in any event, you will not incur any fees as a result of such reimbursement.
4. **Credential Revocation:** Upon reimbursement, any associated license key, Mandatag Entitlement tokens, and service access credentials will be deactivated and revoked.
