# Ugondu Brand Identity & Logo Usage Guidelines

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Product**: Ugondu — Universal Delivery Operating System  
> **Organization**: Air Roofers SASU  
> **Classification**: ENTERPRISE | COMMERCIAL

---

## 1. Primary Logo vs. Compact Symbol

### Primary Logo (`ugondu-logo.svg` / `ugondu-logo.png`)
The primary logo combines the U-shaped initial growth arrow emblem with the official Ugondu wordmark. It is designed for:
- Web navigation headers, landing pages, and marketing sites
- Documentation covers, architecture guides, and user manuals
- Installer splash screens, About dialogs, and platform presentation

### Compact Symbol (`ugondu-symbol.svg` / `ugondu-symbol.png` / `favicon.ico`)
The compact emblem consists solely of the U-shaped upward arrow glyph. It is reserved for:
- Browser favicons and PWA application icons
- Desktop application icons (`app-icon.ico`, `app-icon.png`)
- Compact collapsed sidebars and mobile navigation
- Optional interactive terminal branding

---

## 2. Clear Space, Background & Contrast

- **Minimum Clear Space**: Maintain a clear space around the logo equal to at least 25% of the emblem width. No text, borders, or graphics may encroach upon this zone.
- **Backgrounds**: The logo features genuine transparency. It renders naturally on white, light-neutral, and dark backgrounds.
- **Prohibited Alterations**:
  - Do not alter the blue (`#02438d`) or gold (`#f5ae04`) brand colors.
  - Do not stretch, skew, rotate, or distort proportions.
  - Do not apply artificial drop shadows, glows, or white strokes around the glyph.
  - Do not place the full logo in layouts where text falls below 12px height; use the compact symbol instead.
