# Governance Decision Record: GDR-0002
**Title:** Multidimensional Release Confidence & ORR Evidence Emission
**Date:** 2026-07-17
**Status:** Accepted

## Reason
While GDR-0001 decoupled Compliance from Evidence Confidence, aggregating Confidence into a single index masked specific operational risk profiles (e.g., a simulated linter was weighted equivalently to a simulated security scanner). Additionally, the Operational Readiness Review (ORR) functioned as a procedural gate outside the Event-Driven Policy Graph rather than emitting standardized evidence.

## Impact
1. **Multidimensional Confidence Tracking:** Evidence Confidence is now tracked explicitly per domain (Build, Security, Contracts, etc.). 
2. **Release Confidence:** A new `Release Confidence` score computes the holistic operational risk by weighting the *type* of simulated evidence (e.g., simulated security lowers Release Confidence more aggressively than simulated linting).
3. **ORR Evidence Emission:** The Operational Readiness Review must emit a standardized YAML evidence record into the Registry, proving deployment primitives (manifests, telemetry, rollback) exist.
4. **Immutable GDRs:** A formal principle was established that Governance Decision Records are strictly append-only. No existing GDR may be edited.

## Migration
* `WAVE-1C-EVIDENCE-INGESTION.md` and `WAVE-1C-EVIDENCE-BOOTSTRAP.md` have been updated to display multidimensional confidence matrixes.
* The ORR for `certify` formally emitted an ORR Evidence Record into `WAVE-1C-EVIDENCE-CERTIFY.md`.
